# ICO registration — public launch gate

## Status

**DESIRLY LIMITED is not currently recorded as registered with the Information
Commissioner's Office.**

No registration number may appear on the public privacy notice, in marketing, or
in any document, until registration is confirmed. There is no number to publish,
and inventing one would be both false and trivially checkable against the ICO's
public register.

## Why this blocks launch

Under the Data Protection (Charges and Information) Regulations 2018, an
organisation processing personal data electronically must complete the ICO's fee
self-assessment and, unless exempt, pay the annual data protection fee. DESIRLY
LIMITED processes personal data electronically: account holders, and — once
order sync runs — merchants' customers.

The exemptions are narrow and unlikely to apply to a commercial SaaS platform,
but the self-assessment is the thing that settles it, and it has not been done.

**This is a legal-compliance gate, not an engineering one.** No code change
clears it.

## Required before public customer onboarding or first live seller consent

1. Complete the ICO fee self-assessment for DESIRLY LIMITED (company number
   16381500).
2. If registration is required, register and pay the fee.
3. Record the registration number and renewal date internally.
4. Decide whether to publish the number in the privacy notice. Publishing is
   common and reassuring; it is not strictly mandatory. If published, update the
   notice and this document in the same change.
5. Diarise the annual renewal. A lapsed registration is a live compliance
   failure, not a paperwork one.

## What the public notice says

Nothing about registration status. The notice tells people they may complain to
the ICO and links to `https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/`, which is their
right regardless of whether we are registered.

It deliberately does **not** announce that registration is outstanding. That
would be alarming, would tell a reader nothing useful about how their data is
handled, and is an internal gate rather than a disclosure. It equally does not
claim or imply registration.
